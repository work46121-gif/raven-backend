Not every bill needs
an equal split.

One person covering more of the rental car? Sharing a booking unevenly? Adjust each person’s share in Trip Hub using percentages or dollar amounts.

A SIMPLE EXAMPLE

$600 car booking · 3 people

One person pays $300 (50%).
The other two pay $150 (25%) each.

Try it on your next trip

  1. Open your trip and add or edit a receipt.
  2. Choose Adjust split, then percentages or dollar amounts.
  3. Review the shares, save the split, then save the receipt.
